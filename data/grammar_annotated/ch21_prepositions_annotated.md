# Chapter 21: Prepositions

Prepositions link words and express relationships between them. Key rules:
- When a preposition is followed by a verb, Spanish always uses the **infinitive** — never the gerund (contrast English 'before dancing' → *antes de bailar*, never ~~antes de bailando~~). See 17.2.
- Verbs, nouns, adjectives and past participles used with prepositions must be learned with their appropriate preposition (*abundar en*, *contar con*, *amor a*, *aficionado a*).

## **21.1 *A***

### **21.1.1 The personal *a***

**Rule:** The personal *a* is placed before the **direct object** of a verb when that object refers to a **specific, known person**. When the person is unknown or unspecific, *a* can be omitted.

**Three extensions:**
- **(21.1.1.2)** Also used before nouns indicating a **group of people**, and before person-pronouns (*alguien*, *nadie*, *ninguno*, *usted*, *otro*, *todos*).
- **(21.1.1.3)** Also used for **animals** for which one feels affection — especially pets. NOT used when the animal is treated as food or as an abstract threat (*Prepararon pollo*, *Odia las arañas*).
- **(extended)** Also found before **inanimate objects** after verbs of precedence/superiority (*obedecer*, *preceder*, *suceder*, *seguir*, *superar*, *sustituir*, *vencer*), or to avoid ambiguity in a clause.

*Note: Not used when referring to something inanimate by a person's name: **No conoce todo Vargas Llosa** 'He does not know all (the works of) Vargas Llosa'.*
*When a sentence has both a specific person as direct object AND a personal indirect object, the personal a on the direct object is usually omitted to avoid double *a*.*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| No conozco a Yolanda. | I don't know Yolanda. | specific person — a required |
| Solo nominaron al director. | They only nominated the director. | specific person — a required |
| Necesito jardinero. | I need a gardener. | unspecific — a omitted |
| Ese autobús lleva treinta personas. | That bus is carrying thirty people. | unspecific — a omitted |
| Leyendo ese libro conocí al pueblo chileno. | I came to know the Chilean people. | group of people |
| No vimos a nadie. | We didn't see anyone. | person-pronoun |
| Recomendaron a otro para el puesto. | They recommended another man for the job. | person-pronoun |
| Rescataron al pobre zorro. | They saved the poor fox. | pet/affection |
| Cusqueña supera a sus competidores. | Cusqueña is better than its competitors. | inanimate + superiority verb |
| Nuestro equipo venció a Argentina. | Our team beat Argentina. | inanimate direct object |

**Examples:**

|---|---|
| Van a dar un premio al mejor estudiante. | They are going to give a prize to the best student. |

### **21.1.3 *A* indicating movement**

**Rule:** After verbs of motion, *a* generally corresponds to English 'to', but may also translate 'at', 'in', 'into', 'onto'. Note: **static** position 'in' or 'at' is expressed by *en*, not *a* (except in set phrases like *al lado de*).

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llegamos a Lima hoy. | We arrive in Lima today. |
| Los espectadores tiraron botellas al campo. | The spectators threw bottles onto the pitch. |
| Se sentaron a la mesa para almorzar. | They sat at the table to have lunch. |
| Tiraron las drogas al mar. | They threw the drugs into the sea. |
| Estaremos en la frontera mañana. | We shall be at the frontier tomorrow. (static — *en*) |

### **21.1.4 *A* with expressions of time and age**

**Rule:** *A* usually translates 'at' and 'on' in time expressions. Also found for 'after' (with a time period). Used in many fixed temporal phrases.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Vino a las once y se fue al mediodía. | He came at 11 o'clock and went at midday. |
| A los quince años ya era futbolista. | At fifteen he was already a footballer. |
| A la mañana siguiente ya había pasado la tormenta. | The following morning the storm had passed. |
| A los seis meses de regresar, se enfermó. | Six months after returning, she fell ill. |
| a principios de | at the beginning of |
| a mediados de | in the middle of |
| a la llegada de | on the arrival of |

### **21.1.7 *A* with the meaning of 'from'**

**Rule:** *A* corresponds to 'from' after verbs implying **depriving, taking away or purchasing from**: *quitar*, *cortar*, *robar*, *comprar*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La policía le quitó la ropa al sospechoso. | The police took away the suspect's clothes. |
| Se lo compré a Carlos. | I bought it from Carlos. |

*See 8.2.2.4 for the use of the indirect object pronoun with this meaning.*

### **21.1.8 Expressions constructed with *a***

**Rule:** *A* forms many fixed expressions in four groups:

**(a) Manner phrases:** *a caballo*, *a mano*, *a ciegas*, *a la parrilla/plancha*, *a lápiz*, *a pie*; also fuelling/powering (*una lámpara a querosén*, *una olla a presión*). *Note: means of mechanical transport use **en**: **en avión/tren/carro**.*

**(b) Manner as style:** *al*, *a lo*, *a la* + style: *huevos a la flamenca*, *el pelo a lo Beckham*.

**(c) Location phrases:** *al aire libre*, *al sol*, *a la derecha/izquierda*, *a la entrada*, *a lo lejos*, *a la orilla/orillas de*.

**(d) Repeated-word phrases:** *cara a cara*, *gota a gota*, *poco a poco*, *uno a uno*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| una lámpara a querosén [LA] | a kerosene lamp |
| una olla a presión | a pressure cooker |
| huevos a la flamenca | baked eggs with sausage, ham and tomatoes |
| el pelo a lo Beckham | Beckham-style hair |
| poco a poco | little by little |
| cara a cara | face to face |

## **21.2 *Antes de*, *ante*, *delante de***

**Rule:** Three prepositions translate 'before', but with different meanings:
- **Antes de** → temporal 'before'
- **Ante** → 'before' in the sense of 'in the presence of', or 'faced with' / 'as a result of'
- **Delante de** → locational 'in front of', 'ahead of'

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Preposition |
|---|---|---|
| Se fueron antes de las seis. | They went before six o'clock. | *antes de* — time |
| Deben comparecer ante la comisión. | They must appear before the tribunal. | *ante* — presence |
| Ante las revelaciones el ministro dimitió. | Faced with the revelations the minister resigned. | *ante* — faced with |
| Nos veremos delante de la biblioteca. | We'll meet in front of the library. | *delante de* — location |

## **21.3 *Bajo*, *debajo de***

**Rule:** Both translate 'under(neath)', 'beneath'.
- **Debajo de** (and *abajo de* [LA]) → **literal** physical position
- **Bajo** → literal (less restricted: 'under the sun') and **figurative** (e.g. 'under the command of', 'under the pretext of')

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Puso su bolso debajo/abajo [LA] de la silla. | She put her handbag underneath the chair. | literal position |
| bajo el sol/la lluvia/el cielo | under the sun/rain/sky | bajo — literal, unrestricted |
| bajo el pretexto de estar enfermo | under the pretext of being ill | bajo — figurative |

## **21.4 *Con***

**Rule:** Basic meaning is 'with' ('accompanied by', instrument 'with', means 'by', adverbial). Extended uses: (i) encounter/collision/struggle verbs (*tropezar con*, *enfrentarse con*); (ii) cause; (iii) feelings/attitudes 'towards'; (iv) 'in spite of', 'despite'.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Maribel sale con Paco. | Maribel is going out with Paco. | accompanied by |
| Lograron taparlo con un parche. | They managed to cover it with a patch. | instrument |
| Lo saludaron con entusiasmo. | They greeted him enthusiastically. | adverbial manner |
| Al pasar por la curva la moto chocó con un farol. | The motorcycle crashed into a lamp post. | encounter/collision |
| Con pintar las paredes la casa ya parece otra. | Just by painting the walls the house seems transformed. | cause |
| Es simpático con todo el mundo. | He is friendly towards everyone. | attitude towards |
| Con todo el dinero que tiene nunca invita. | In spite of all his money he never buys you a drink. | despite |

## **21.5 *Contra*, *en contra de***

**Rule:** **Contra** serves for most literal and figurative translations of 'against', and for firing weapons 'at'. **En contra de** is sometimes preferred to express an opinion or feeling.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Coloca la escalera contra el muro. | Place the ladder against the wall. |
| Dirigen sus misiles contra la ciudad. | They are aiming their missiles at the city. |
| Está en contra de los toros. | He is against bullfighting. |

## **21.6 *De***

**Rule:** *De* is one of the most versatile prepositions. Core meaning: origin/source and 'of' for ownership, relationship and composition. Many further uses below.

### 21.6.1–21.6.9 Uses of *de*

| Use | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **21.6.1 Origin / point of departure** | 'from', or 'in' in superlatives | *el mejor hotel de Río* — the best hotel in Rio |
| **21.6.2 Ownership / relationship** | equivalent to English 's | *los primos de José* — José's cousins; *de* + personal name = disdainful: *el idiota de Juan* |
| **21.6.3 Geographical names** | island of, track to | *la isla de Aruba*, *el camino de Machu Picchu* |
| **21.6.4 Material / contents** | made of, containing | *una caja de madera* — a wooden box |
| **21.6.5 Topic ('about')** | talking/writing about | *Están hablando de su visita* — talking about their visit (formal treatment → *sobre*) |
| **21.6.6 Cause** | of, with | *Falleció de cólera* — he died of cholera; *loco de alegría* |
| **21.6.7 'As' or 'like'** | actual occupation, assumed role, stage of life, use | *Lole trabaja de azafata* — works as an air hostess; *De niño vivía en otro país* |
| **21.6.8 Adjectival phrases** | descriptive or intended use | *una niña de ojos azules* — a girl with blue eyes |
| **21.6.9 Adverbial phrases** | fixed adverbial expressions | *de golpe* suddenly, *de nuevo* again, *de paso* in passing |

**Cross-references for other uses of *de*:** comparisons (26.4), time/dates (23.1.5.1/2), before infinitives (17.2.1(b), 17.2.5.2, 17.4), agent after past participle (15.1.2), compound nouns (29.3.2).

## **21.7 *Dentro de*, *fuera de***

**Rule:**
- **Dentro de** → '(with)in', 'inside' for location; 'within' for future time. *Adentro de* [LA] indicates location (not generally accepted in Spain).
- **Fuera de** / *afuera de* [LA] → 'outside'

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La ciudad antigua está dentro de las murallas. | The ancient city is within the walls. |
| El avión llega dentro de media hora. | The plane arrives in half an hour. |

## **21.8 *Desde***

**Rule:** *Desde* translates 'from' — especially to emphasise distance and range, or to avoid confusion with *de*. Also translates time 'from' or 'since'. In **desde ... hasta**, more emphatic than *de ... a*.

*See 11.4.2 for **desde** + **hace/hacía** with ongoing past actions.*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Viajó desde Roma hasta Barcelona para verla. | He travelled all the way from Rome to Barcelona to see her. |
| camisas desde diez hasta cien dólares | shirts from ten to a hundred dollars |
| Estamos buscando casa desde febrero. | We have been looking for a house since February. |
| La conoce desde niña. | He has known her since childhood. |

## **21.9 *Detrás de*, *tras***

**Rule:**
- **Detrás de** (and *atrás de* [LA]) → 'behind', 'after' with reference to **position**
- **Tras** → literary/journalistic; also for **temporal succession** ('after') and after verbs of motion ('run after'). Repeated-word expressions: *día tras día*. *Note: In Latin America, these repeated expressions often use **a**: **día a día**.*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Qué hay detrás de la casa? | What's behind the house? | position |
| Alguien esperaba tras la cortina. | Someone was waiting behind the curtain. | tras — literary |
| día tras día | day after day | temporal repetition |
| Tras las lluvias vienen las inundaciones. | After the rains come the floods. | temporal succession |
| Isabel echó a andar tras él. | Isabel went off after him. | motion 'after' |

## **21.10 *En*, *encima de*, *sobre***

**Rule:** Three prepositions for 'in/on/above'. **En** covers 'in(side)', 'at' and 'into' — both literal and figurative. **Sobre** and **encima de** are preferable when *en* would be ambiguous.

| Use | Preposition(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 'in(side)', 'at', 'into' | **en** | Covers all: *en el bolsillo*, *en casa*, *en el garaje*. In LA, *entrar a* [LA] replaces *entrar en* |
| '(up)on', 'on' | **en**, **sobre**, **encima de** | *En* for clear cases (*en mi hombro*). *Sobre/encima de* when *en* could mean 'inside' |
| 'on top of' (greater/unexpected height) | **encima de** (preferred) | *encima del armario* — on top of the cupboard |
| 'above', 'over' (literal) | **sobre**, **encima de** | *volar sobre los Alpes* |
| 'above', 'over' (figurative) | **sobre**, **encima de** | 'on top of everything', 'misfortune on misfortune' |
| superiority, precedence, magnitude | **sobre** | *sobre todo* — above all; *temperatura sobre cero* |
| 'around', 'approximately' (time) | **sobre** | *Llego sobre las dos* |
| 'on', 'about', 'concerning' (topic) | **sobre** | *una conferencia sobre Brasil* (formal; see also 21.6.5) |

**Key contrast:** *Los vimos en la televisión* (on TV, watching it) vs *Lo puso sobre/encima de la televisión* (on top of the TV set).

**Expressions with *en*:** *en broma* (as a joke), *en casa* (at home), *en caso de* (in case), *en efecto* (indeed), *en lugar de* (instead of), *en resumen* (in short), *enseguida* (right away), *en vez de* (instead of).

## **21.11 *Enfrente de*, *frente a***

**Rule:** Both correspond to 'opposite', 'in front of'. **Frente a** is also used with the meaning of 'in the face of', 'in opposition to'.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| el hotel enfrente de la estación | the hotel in front of the station |
| frente al museo | opposite the museum |
| El Osasuna perdió frente a Valencia. | Osasuna lost against Valencia. |

## **21.12 *Entre***

**Rule:** **Entre** generally means 'between', 'among(st)'. Also used collectively ('between them / together'). *Entre semana* = 'on weekdays'. Note: *entre* takes **subject pronouns** (*tú*, *yo* — not *ti*, *mí*) — see 8.1.2.2.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No pude decidir entre protestar y callarme. | I could not decide between protesting and keeping quiet. |
| La escritura se halla entre otros papeles. | The deed can be found amongst other papers. |
| Entre Jorge, Luis y Antonio lograron formar un equipo. | Between them, Jorge, Luis and Antonio managed to form a team. |
| Solo se permite estacionar entre semana. | Parking is only permitted on weekdays. |

## **21.13 *Hacia*, *hasta***

**Rule:**
- **Hacia** → 'towards' (literal and figurative); also 'around/about' for approximate time or place
- **Hasta** → 'until', 'as far as', 'up to' (time and place); also forms farewell expressions. *For **hasta** meaning 'not until' [LA], see 30.6.3.3.*

**Farewell expressions with *hasta*:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hasta ahora | see you in a minute |
| hasta luego | see you, bye |
| hasta mañana | see you tomorrow |
| hasta entonces | see you then |
| hasta pronto | see you soon |
| hasta la vista | so long |
| hasta siempre | goodbye |
| hasta la vuelta | see you when I/you get back |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Preposition |
|---|---|---|
| La carretera se desvía hacia Chipiona. | The road branches off to Chipiona. | hacia — direction |
| No comparto su actitud hacia la guerra. | I do not share his attitude towards war. | hacia — figurative |
| Esperan que salga el tren hacia las cuatro. | They hope the train leaves around 4 o'clock. | hacia — approximation |
| El libro estudia el tema hasta 1950. | The book studies the topic up to 1950. | hasta — up to |
| La valla llega hasta el río. | The fence runs as far as the river. | hasta — as far as |

## **21.14 *Según***

**Rule:** **Según** usually means 'according to', 'depending on'. Used alone in speech: 'it depends'. Before verbs of saying/affirming: 'according to what'. Before other verbs: implies *mientras* / *a medida que* 'as', 'whilst'. Takes **subject pronouns** (*yo*, *tú*) — see 8.1.2.2.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Hay que montarlo según las instrucciones. | You have to set it up according to the instructions. | according to |
| Iremos o no según el tiempo. | We shall go or not depending on the weather. | depending on |
| ¿Piensas ir mañana? Según. | Do you intend to go tomorrow? It depends. | standalone in speech |
| según afirman algunos científicos | according to what some scientists state | before verb of saying |
| Apunta las notas según las voy diciendo. | Write the marks down as I read them out. | 'as/whilst' |

## **21.16 Combinations of prepositions**

**Rule:** Prepositions frequently combine using **de**, **desde** and **por**. **Por** is especially used to add an idea of **movement** to compound prepositions.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El agua salía de entre las tablas. | The water came out from between the boards. |
| Desde detrás de la pared se oía música. | From behind the wall music could be heard. |
| El niño se escondió por debajo de la cama. | The boy hid under the bed. |
| La autopista pasa casi por encima de su casa. | The motorway passes almost over their house. |

## **21.17 *Cuando* and *donde* used as prepositions**

**Rule:** Although normally relative adverbs (see 25.6.2), in everyday Spanish *cuando* and *donde* are frequently used effectively as **prepositions** — meaning 'as a' / 'during' and 'near' / 'at (someone's place)' respectively.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Venía aquí mucho cuando niña. | I often came here as a girl. |
| Eso pasaba mucho cuando la guerra. | This happened a lot during the war. |
| Viven donde Eduardo y Ana. | They live near Eduardo and Ana. |
