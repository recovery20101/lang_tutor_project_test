# Chapter 26: Comparative and superlative constructions

**Comparative constructions** express:
- **Inequality:** 'Peter is taller than John', 'more/less liberal than'
- **Equality:** 'Peter is as tall/liberal as John'

**Superlative constructions** establish limits: 'the tallest', 'the most liberal'.

**Key Spanish words:** *más* (more), *menos* (less), *tan* (as), *tanto/a/os/as* (as much/many), link words *que*, *de*, *como*.

## **26.1 Comparisons involving adjectives or adverbs**

### **26.1.1 Basic pattern**

**Rule:**
- **Inequality:** *más/menos* + adjective/adverb + **que**
- **Equality:** *tan* + adjective/adverb + **como**

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pedro es más/menos alto que Luis. | Pedro is taller/shorter than Luis. |
| Pedro es tan alto como Luis. | Pedro is as tall as Luis. |
| Pedro conduce más/menos despacio que Luis. | Pedro drives more/less slowly than Luis. |
| Pedro conduce tan despacio como Luis. | Pedro drives as slowly as Luis. |

### **26.1.2 Irregular comparatives**

#### 26.1.2.1 Forms

**Rule:** The following adjectives and adverbs have single-word comparative forms. The regular forms (*más bueno*, *más malo*, *más bien*, *más mal*) are usually replaced by *mejor/peor*. The adjective forms are invariable for gender but have plural **-es**. The adverb forms are invariable.

| Adjective | Regular | Irregular | Adverb | Regular | Irregular |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bueno (good) | más bueno | **mejor** (better) | bien (well) | más bien | **mejor** (better) |
| malo (bad) | más malo | **peor** (worse) | mal (badly) | más mal | **peor** (worse) |
| grande (big) | más grande | **mayor** (greater/older) | — | — | — |
| pequeño (small) | más pequeño | **menor** (lesser/younger) | — | — | — |

*Notes:*
- *Más bien* = 'rather': *Su mirada era más bien triste* 'She had a rather sad look'.
- *Más bueno/malo* are used for **moral** qualities: *ser más bueno que el pan* 'to be very good'.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi bicicleta es mejor que la tuya. | My bike is better than yours. |
| Estos niños son menores que los míos. | These children are younger than mine. |
| Cantan mejor que yo. | They sing better than me. |

#### 26.1.2.2 *Mayor/menor* vs *más grande/más pequeño*

**Rule:**
- *Más grande* / *más pequeño* → **physical size**
- *Mayor* → **age** or **abstract degree/intensity/importance** ('greater', 'higher', 'elder')
- *Menor* → **age** or **abstract comparisons**; rarely used for physical size

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Mi casa es más grande/pequeña que la tuya. | My house is bigger/smaller than yours. | physical size |
| Pedro es mayor/menor que Luis. | Pedro is older/younger than Luis. | age |
| una persona mayor | an elderly person | age |
| El índice de paro es mayor allí que aquí. | Unemployment is higher there than here. | abstract degree |
| la Calle Mayor | the High Street | importance |
| Ahora viene con menor frecuencia. | Now she comes less often. | abstract |
| Se vende en cantidades cada vez menores. | It sells in smaller and smaller quantities. | abstract |

## **26.2 Comparisons involving nouns**

### **26.2.1 Inequality with nouns**

**Rule:** Same structure as for adjectives: *más/menos* + noun + *que*. When *mucho* is used, it **agrees** with the associated noun.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pedro comió más/menos patatas que tú. | Pedro ate more/fewer potatoes than you. |
| Hace más calor en Sevilla que en Madrid. | It's hotter in Seville than in Madrid. |
| Comió muchas más patatas que tú. | He ate many more potatoes than you. |
| mucha menos velocidad | much less speed |

### **26.2.2 Equality with nouns**

**Rule:** Equality with nouns: *tanto/a/os/as* + noun + *como* ('as much/many ... as'). Note: *tanto ... que* (with no *como*) = **result** ('so much/many ... that').

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pedro tiene tantas camisas como Luis. | Pedro has as many shirts as Luis. |
| No hace tanto calor en Cantabria. | It isn't so hot in Cantabria. |
| Bebió tantas copas que se emborrachó. | He drank so many glasses that he got drunk. |

## **26.3 Comparisons involving verbs**

**Rule:**
- **Inequality:** verb + *más/menos* + *que*
- **Equality:** verb + *tanto* (invariable) + *como*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Susana trabaja más/menos que tú. | Susana works more/less than you. |
| Susana trabaja tanto como tú. | Susana works as much as you. |

## **26.4 Uses of *de* after *más* and *menos***

### **26.4.1 *De* before numbers and quantities**

**Rule:** *De* must replace *que* as 'than' after *más/menos* when comparing against a **number or numerical expression** (half, dozen, etc.) or words implying a quantity.

**Critical distinction:** *no más de* = 'not more than' (limit); *no más que* = 'only' (restriction).

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No vengas más tarde de las ocho. | Don't come any later than eight o'clock. |
| Murieron menos de la mitad de los pasajeros. | Fewer than half the passengers died. |
| Vino menos gente de la esperada. | Fewer people than expected came. |
| No distribuyeron más de diez kilos. | They did not distribute more than ten kilos. |
| No distribuyeron más que diez kilos. | They only distributed ten kilos. |

### **26.4.2 *Más/menos de* before a clause**

**Rule:** When the comparative precedes a **subordinate clause**, *de* replaces *que*:

- If comparison refers to a **specific noun** in the main clause: *más/menos + del/de la/de los/de las + que* (agreeing in gender/number with the noun).
- If comparison refers to the **main clause as a whole**: *más/menos + de lo que* ('than what').

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Tiene más dinero del que necesita. | He has more money than he needs. | specific noun (masc. sg.) → *del que* |
| Hubo menos problemas de los que esperaba. | There were fewer problems than I expected. | specific noun (masc. pl.) → *de los que* |
| Has comprado más carpetas de las que necesitamos. | You've bought more folders than we need. | specific noun (fem. pl.) → *de las que* |
| Pedro es más listo de lo que creíamos. | Pedro is cleverer than we thought. | whole clause → *de lo que* |
| Elena gana menos de lo que pensaba. | Elena earns less than I thought. | whole clause → *de lo que* |

## **26.5 *Cuanto más* and *cuanto menos* — 'the more ... the more'**

**Rule:** English 'the more/less ... the more/less' = **cuanto más/menos ... (tanto) más/menos ...**. *Cuanto* and *tanto* agree with any associated noun. *Tanto* is nowadays usually omitted from the second part. Irregular comparatives may replace *más/menos*. In speech, *cuanto* is often replaced by *mientras*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Cuantos más trabajos corregimos ahora menos tendremos que hacer esta noche. | The more assignments we mark now, the fewer we'll have to do tonight. |
| Cuanto más lo pienso menos me apetece. | The more I think about it the less I fancy it. |
| Mientras más lo toques más te va a picar. | The more you touch it the more it will itch. |

## **26.6 Superlative constructions**

### **26.6.1 Superlative adjectives**

**Rule:** English '-est' or 'most' = **definite article/possessive + noun + comparative adjective**. 'In' is translated by **de**. The noun can be omitted when clear from context. The definite article is **not** used after verbs meaning 'to be', 'to seem', 'to look', 'to become'.

*Note: Unlike French, Spanish does NOT repeat the article before the comparative ('el chico más inteligente', not ~~'el chico el más inteligente'~~).*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| la montaña más alta de España | the highest mountain in Spain |
| nuestros productos más solicitados | our most sought-after products |
| su mayor éxito | her greatest success |
| No compres la más cara. | Don't buy the most expensive one. (noun omitted) |
| Es en verano cuando el agua del mar está más caliente. | It's in the summer that the sea is warmest. (no article — with *estar*) |
| Así es como queda más bonito. | That's the way it looks nicest. (no article — with *quedar*) |

### **26.6.2 Superlative adverbs**

**Rule:** Superlative adverbs use *más/menos* + adverb (or an irregular form) WITHOUT the definite article. **Exception:** if the adverb is qualified by a relative clause or adjective, use **lo**: *lo más despacio que puedas*. Also in: *lo más temprano/tarde posible*, *lo antes posible*.

For 'works hardest', 'eats the most' — use *más* (not *lo*): *Ana es la que más trabaja*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es Pedro quien conduce más despacio. | It's Pedro who drives slowest. |
| El que baila peor es Antonio. | The one who dances worst is Antonio. |
| Conduce lo más despacio que puedas. | Drive as slowly as you can. |
| Ven lo más pronto posible. | Come as soon as possible. |
| Ana es la que más trabaja. | Ana is the one who works hardest. |
| La que más come es Pepi. | The one who eats the most is Pepi. |

### **26.6.3 Absolute superlatives — *-ísimo***

**Rule:** The suffix **-ísimo** intensifies an adjective ('very' + adjective). Adjective forms agree in gender and number with the noun. Formation rules: consonant-ending → add *-ísimo* directly; single vowel → drop vowel + *-ísimo*; two vowels → drop last vowel (or both if not forming two syllables); spelling changes for *-co* → *-quísimo*, *-go* → *-guísimo*, *-z* → *-císimo*; *-ble* → *-bil-* + *-ísimo*.

Any written accent on the original adjective is **removed**.

**Cannot be added to all adjectives** — when in doubt, use *muy*, *sumamente*, *extremadamente*, *altamente*, *excesivamente* instead.

**Formation examples:**

| Adjective | Absolute superlative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| liberal | liberalísimo | very liberal |
| rápido | rapidísimo | very fast |
| limpio | limpísimo | extra clean |
| feo | feísimo | extremely ugly |
| poco | poquísimo | very little |
| largo | larguísimo | very long |
| feliz | felicísimo | very happy |
| amable | amabilísimo | very friendly |
| antiguo | antiquísimo | very old, ancient |
| joven | jovencísimo | very young |
| malo | pésimo | very bad, dreadful |
| fiel | fidelísimo | very faithful |

**Máximo/mínimo** (non-*-ísimo* forms): *su máxima ambición* 'her greatest ambition', *la tarifa mínima* 'the minimum charge'.

### **26.6.4 The suffix *-ísimamente***

**Rule:** *-ísimamente* can be added to adjectives (infrequently) to form an intensified adverb: 'very/most ... -ly'. Formed like *-mente* adverbs from the *-ísimo* form.

**Examples:**

| Adjective | Adverb | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| rápido | rapidísimamente | very rapidly |
| claro | clarísimamente | most clearly |

## **26.7 Other comparative expressions**

**Rule:** Fixed comparative expressions for specific meanings.

| Construction | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *no más que* | only | *Juan no habla más que de política.* — Juan only talks about politics. |
| *nada más que* | nothing except | *No había nada más que dos bollitos.* — There was nothing except two bread rolls. |
| *nadie más que* | nobody except | *No vimos a nadie más que a Pedro.* — We saw no one except Pedro. |
| *cada vez más/menos* | more and more / less and less | *El tren iba cada vez más rápido.* — The train went faster and faster. |

## **26.8 Expressions of equality — 'the same (thing) as'**

**Rule:** English 'as' after 'same' is translated by **que**. The word for 'same' varies:
- **mismo** + *que* — before nouns
- **igual** + *que* — after verbs
- **igual de** + adjective + *que* — before adjectives
- **lo mismo** + *que* — 'the same (thing) as'

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tiene el mismo televisor que nosotros. | He has the same television set as us. |
| Ana es igual que su madre. | Ana is just like her mother. |
| Es igual de antipático que su hermano. | He's as unpleasant as his brother. |
| Dijo lo mismo que Luis. | He said the same thing as Luis. |
