# Chapter 20: *Ser* and *estar*

Spanish has two verbs equivalent to English 'to be': **ser** and **estar**. They are not interchangeable — the distinction is in principle clear:

- **Ser** → categorises its subject (what it is, what type/class it belongs to)
- **Estar** → assigns acquired properties (conditions, states) or locates persons/things in space

**Practical tests:**
- Can you insert 'in a ... state/condition'? → **estar**
- Can you insert 'person', 'type', 'variety', 'sort'? → **ser**
- Can 'to be' be replaced by 'to seem', 'to feel', 'to become' or 'to look'? → **estar**

## **20.1 Situations which demand *ser***

### **20.1.1 *Ser* linking subject to a noun, pronoun or infinitive**

**Rule:** Use *ser* when 'to be' links the subject to a **noun**, **pronoun** or **infinitive**.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| (El) Perú es una república. | Peru is a republic. |
| Soy el nuevo jefe. | I am the new boss. |
| Este es el mío. | This is mine. |
| Su sueño es jugar para el Real Madrid. | His dream is to play for Real Madrid. |

### **20.1.2 *Ser* for origin**

**Rule:** Use *ser* to indicate the **origin** of persons or things — nationality, regional origin, family background.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi madre es de Brasil. | My mother is from Brazil. |
| Juan es andaluz. | Juan is Andalusian. |
| Es de una familia pobre. | She is from a poor family. |

### **20.1.7 *Ser* for location of events**

**Rule:** Use *ser* to state **where an event takes place**. Contrast with *estar* (20.2.1) which locates persons and things.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La clase será en el aula grande. | The class will be in the large classroom. |
| Aquí es donde se cayó. | This is where she fell. |

## **20.2 Situations which demand *estar***

### **20.2.1 *Estar* for location or position**

**Rule:** Use *estar* to express **location or position** of persons and things — whether temporary or permanent. Contrast with *ser* (20.1.7) which states where an event happens.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sus papeles estaban sobre la mesa. | His papers were on the table. |
| Lima está en (el) Perú. | Lima is in Peru. |

### **20.2.2 *Estar* to form progressive tenses**

**Rule:** *Estar* is used as the auxiliary in all **progressive / continuous tenses** (*estar* + gerund). See 10.10 and 11.3.

**Example:** *Está lloviendo.* 'It is raining.'

## **20.3 *Ser* and *estar* with adjectives**

### **20.3.1 *Ser* + adjective — inherent or essential characteristics**

**Rule:** *Ser* + adjective indicates an **inherent or essential characteristic** — putting someone or something into a class or type. It is 'to be by nature', with no idea of change.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana es pálida. | Ana is pale (normally of pale complexion). |
| Estas peras son agrias. | These pears are sour (of a sour variety). |
| Mi hermano es alegre. | My brother is happy (of cheerful disposition). |
| La puerta es roja. | The door is red (inherent colour). |
| Luis es muy mentiroso. | Luis is a liar (character trait). |
| Esta marca de salsa es picante. | This brand of sauce is spicy (by nature). |

### **20.3.2 *Estar* + adjective — accidental or temporary states**

**Rule:** *Estar* + adjective denotes an **accidental or temporary state** — 'to be by condition'. This indicates not only that a condition may change, but also that it is **the result of a change**. If 'to be' can be replaced by 'to seem', 'to feel', 'to become' or 'to look' → use *estar*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana está pálida. | Ana is pale (has turned pale). |
| Estas peras están agrias. | These pears are sour (because they are unripe). |
| Mi hermano está alegre. | My brother is happy (in a happy state of mind). |
| Está vivo. | He is alive. |
| Está muerto. | He is dead. |
| Está calvo. | He is bald. |
| La puerta está sucia. | The door is dirty (current state). |
| Luis está muy triste. | Luis is very sad (current state). |
| Estuve orgulloso al recibir el premio. | I was proud on receiving the prize. (felt proud) |
| ¡Qué alta está la niña! | How tall the girl is! (how tall she has become/looks) |
| ¡Qué picante está la sopa! | The soup is really spicy! (current condition) |

### **20.3.3 Adjectives that change meaning with *ser* or *estar***

**Rule:** A number of common adjectives have **different meanings** depending on whether they are used with *ser* (inherent characteristic) or *estar* (state/condition).

| Adjective | With *ser* | With *estar* |
|---|---|---|
| cansado | tiresome, tiring | tired |
| listo | clever | ready |
| malo | bad | ill |
| bueno | good | well, tasty (food) |
| vivo | lively, clever | alive |
| aburrido | boring | bored |
| divertido | amusing | amused |
| fresco | cheeky | cool (e.g. a drink) |
| consciente | aware | conscious [SP] |
| rico | rich | tasty [SP] |

### **20.3.4 Common adjectives predominantly with *ser* or *estar***

**Rule:** Some adjectives are associated predominantly with one verb.

**Predominantly with *ser*:** *ser viejo/joven* (age as inherent trait — though *estar viejo/joven* implies change or appearance); *ser fácil/difícil*, *ser posible/probable*, *ser evidente*, *ser necesario*, *ser importante*, *ser conveniente*, *ser increíble*; *ser feliz*, *ser desgraciado*.

*Note: **está claro que** [SP] 'It's clear that' is an exception.*

**Predominantly with *estar*:** *estar lleno*, *estar vacío*, *estar intacto*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| Es una persona muy feliz. | She is a very happy person. | *ser* — disposition |
| Estoy muy viejo para estos trotes. | I'm getting too old for all this. | *estar* — change perceived |
| Está joven para su edad. | She looks young for her age. | *estar* — appearance |
| Es difícil hacerlo. | It's difficult to do it. | *ser* |
| Está llena la sala. | The room is full. | *estar* |

### CMP: *Ser* vs *estar* — full reference

**Rule:** Summary of all contexts requiring *ser* or *estar*.

| Context | *Ser* | *Estar* |
|---|---|---|
| Links subject to noun/pronoun/infinitive | ✓ | ✗ |
| Origin, nationality | ✓ | ✗ |
| Ownership / possession | ✓ | ✗ |
| Material composition | ✓ | ✗ |
| Mathematical calculations | ✓ | ✗ |
| Time, date, seasons | ✓ | ✗ |
| Location of **events** | ✓ | ✗ |
| Location of **persons/things** | ✗ | ✓ |
| Progressive tenses | ✗ | ✓ |
| Inherent / essential adjective | ✓ | ✗ |
| Temporary / accidental state adjective | ✗ | ✓ |
| Passive voice (+ past participle) | ✓ | ✗ |
| Resulting state (+ past participle) | ✗ | ✓ |

**Key contrast pairs:**

| *Ser* | Meaning | *Estar* | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ana es pálida. | Ana is pale (by nature). | Ana está pálida. | Ana has turned pale. |
| La clase es en el aula. | The class is held in the room. | Lima está en el Perú. | Lima is located in Peru. |
| Es médico. | He is a doctor. | Está de médico. | He is acting as a doctor. |

## **20.4 *Ser* and *estar* with past participles**

**Rule:** *Ser* + past participle = **passive voice** (action being performed). *Estar* + past participle = **resulting state**. See 15.1 for full details.

| Spanish | English | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| La puerta fue abierta por una criada. | The door was opened by a maid. | *ser* — action |
| La puerta estaba abierta. | The door was open (state). | *estar* — state |

## **20.5 Special uses of *estar***

### **20.5.1 *Estar de* — acting in a capacity**

**Rule:** **Estar de** (not bare *estar*) + noun indicates that a person is **acting in a particular capacity** or **occupying a position temporarily**. Also used in fixed prepositional phrases.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Está de cónsul en Newcastle. | He is acting as consul in Newcastle. |
| Estoy de jefe hasta que vuelva Juan. | I am the boss until Juan returns. |
| estar de vacaciones | on holiday |
| estar de buen/mal humor | in a good/bad mood |
| estar de viaje | travelling |

### **20.5.2 Colloquial expressions using *estar* + noun**

**Rule:** *Estar* very occasionally combines with a **noun** in colloquial expressions — contrary to the general rule that *ser* is used with nouns.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy pez en matemáticas. | I'm clueless about maths. |
| Está fenómeno. | It's fantastic. |
