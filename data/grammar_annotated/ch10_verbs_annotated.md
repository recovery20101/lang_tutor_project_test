# Chapter 10: Verb forms

Spanish verbs are organized into three main classes depending on whether the infinitive ends in **-ar**, **-er** or **-ir**. Each verb form divides into a **stem** (often unchanged) and an **ending** (which changes to express tense, person, number and mood).

The verbs **hablar** 'speak', **beber** 'drink' and **subir** 'climb' are used throughout to illustrate regular patterns. For the meanings and uses of tenses, see Chapter 11.

**General features of verb endings:**
- 2nd person singular ends in **-s**
- 2nd person plural ends in **-is/-ís**
- 1st person plural ends in **-mos**
- 3rd person plural ends in **-n**
- Latin American **vos** forms exist only in the present indicative and singular imperative; in other tenses **vos** uses the same forms as **tú**.

## **10.1 Present indicative and present subjunctive**

The present subjunctive endings are predictable from the present indicative: **-ar** verbs take **e** in the subjunctive ending; **-er/-ir** verbs take **a**. The 1st and 3rd person singular of the present subjunctive are always identical: *(yo) hable*, *(él) hable*.

### **10.1.1 Regular present indicative and present subjunctive**

**Formation note:** Indicative endings: **-ar** → -o/-as/-a/-amos/-áis/-an; **-er** → -o/-es/-e/-emos/-éis/-en; **-ir** → -o/-es/-e/-imos/-ís/-en. In the **-er** and **-ir** conjugations the endings are identical except in the 1st and 2nd persons plural indicative and with **vos**.

**Table 10.1 — Regular present indicative:**

| Person | Hablar 'speak' | Beber 'drink' | Subir 'go up' |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | hablo | bebo | subo |
| 2 (tú) | hablas | bebes | subes |
| (vos) [LA] | hablás | bebés | subís |
| 3 (él etc.) | habla | bebe | sube |
| 1 (nosotros) | hablamos | bebemos | subimos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | habláis | bebéis | subís |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | hablan | beben | suben |

**Table 10.2 — Regular present subjunctive:**

| Person | Hablar | Beber | Subir |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | hable | beba | suba |
| 2 (tú/vos) | hables | bebas | subas |
| 3 (él etc.) | hable | beba | suba |
| 1 (nosotros) | hablemos | bebamos | subamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | habléis | bebáis | subáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | hablen | beban | suban |

#### 10.1.2.1 Radical changing verbs

**Rule:** In some verbs the final stem vowel changes when spoken stress falls on the stem (i.e. in the 1st, 2nd excluding *vos*, and 3rd persons singular, and 3rd person plural). The change **never** occurs in the 1st or 2nd persons plural. Affected vowels: **e** > **ie**, **o** > **ue**.

**Table 10.3 — -ar and -er radical changes (indicative):**

| Person | Pensar 'think' | Volver 'return' |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | pienso | vuelvo |
| 2 (tú) | piensas | vuelves |
| (vos) [LA] | pensás | volvés |
| 3 (él etc.) | piensa | vuelve |
| 1 (nosotros) | pensamos | volvemos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | pensáis | volvéis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | piensan | vuelven |

**Table 10.4 — -ar and -er radical changes (subjunctive):**

| Person | Pensar | Volver |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | piense | vuelva |
| 2 (tú/vos) | pienses | vuelvas |
| 3 (él etc.) | piense | vuelva |
| 1 (nosotros) | pensemos | volvamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | penséis | volváis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | piensen | vuelvan |

Other common verbs with **e > ie**: cerrar, comenzar, confesar, defender, negar, perder, recomendar, and compounds.
Other common verbs with **o > ue**: acordarse, acostarse, costar, encontrar, mostrar, poder, recordar, resolver, soler, soñar, volar, and compounds.

*Notes:*
- **tener** and **venir** also follow this pattern (except their 1st person singular *tengo/vengo*).
- **oler** adds **h** when **o** > **ue**: *huelo*, *hueles* etc.
- **jugar** (u > ue) and **adquirir** (i > ie) follow the same pattern with different vowels.
- A stressed **ie** or **ue** in a derived noun often signals a radical-changing verb: *recuerdo* (recordar), *sueño* (soñar).

**-ir conjugation — Pattern I (e > ie / o > ue, plus e > i or o > u in subjunctive plural):**

In the present subjunctive 1st and 2nd persons plural, **-ir** Pattern I verbs make an additional change: **e > i** (or **o > u** for *morir/dormir*).

**Table 10.5 — -ir radical changes Pattern I:**

| Person | Sentir 'feel' — Indicative | Sentir — Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | siento | sienta |
| 2 (tú) | sientes | sientas |
| (vos) [LA] | sentís | sientas |
| 3 (él etc.) | siente | sienta |
| 1 (nosotros) | sentimos | sintamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | sentís | sintáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | sienten | sientan |

Other Pattern I verbs: advertir, arrepentirse, consentir, convertir, divertir(se), herir, hervir, mentir, preferir, referir(se), sugerir.

**-ir conjugation — Pattern II (e > i throughout stressed forms and all subjunctive):**

**Table 10.6 — -ir radical changes Pattern II:**

| Person | Medir 'measure' — Indicative | Medir — Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | mido | mida |
| 2 (tú) | mides | midas |
| (vos) [LA] | medís | midas |
| 3 (él etc.) | mide | mida |
| 1 (nosotros) | medimos | midamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | medís | midáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | miden | midan |

Other Pattern II verbs: competir, concebir, corregir, derretir(se), elegir, gemir, pedir, rendirse, repetir, seguir, servir, vestir(se), and compounds.

*Note:* **freír** and **reír** follow Pattern II but require a written accent when **i** is stressed: *frío*, *fríes* etc.

#### 10.1.2.2 Consonant changing verbs

**Rule:** Some verbs insert or change a consonant at the end of the stem. When the irregularity affects the 1st person singular present indicative, it carries over to the entire present subjunctive (which is based on it). These changes **never** occur in the 1st or 2nd persons plural of the present indicative.

**Insertion or change to g** — In a small but important group of -er/-ir verbs, **g** is inserted (or replaces **c**) in the 1st person singular present indicative and throughout the present subjunctive:

**Table 10.7 — Insertion of g (poner):**

| Person | Poner 'put' — Indicative | Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | pongo | ponga |
| 2 (tú) | pones | pongas |
| (vos) [LA] | ponés | — |
| 3 (él etc.) | pone | ponga |
| 1 (nosotros) | ponemos | pongamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | ponéis | pongáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | ponen | pongan |

Other verbs with g-insertion: salir (*salgo*), tener (*tengo*), venir (*vengo*), valer (*valgo*).
**decir** (*digo*) and **hacer** (*hago*) replace **c** with **g**.
**caer**, **traer**, **oír** follow the poner pattern but change the stem vowel: *caigo*, *traigo*, *oigo*.

**Verbs in -ucir and -ecer** — Change **c** > **zc** in 1st person singular indicative and throughout the present subjunctive:

| Person | Conducir 'drive' — Indicative | Subjunctive | Aparecer 'appear' — Indicative | Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | conduzco | conduzca | aparezco | aparezca |
| 2 (tú) | conduces | conduzcas | apareces | aparezcas |
| (vos) [LA] | conducís | conduzcas | aparecés | aparezcas |
| 3 (él etc.) | conduce | conduzca | aparece | aparezca |
| 1 (nosotros) | conducimos | conduzcamos | aparecemos | aparezcamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | conducís | conduzcáis | aparecéis | aparezcáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | conducen | conduzcan | aparecen | aparezcan |

Other -ucir/-ecer verbs: conocer, deducir, florecer, introducir, lucir, merecer, nacer, padecer, producir.

**Verbs in -uir** — Insert **y** in all persons of the present indicative except 1st and 2nd plural, and throughout the present subjunctive:

**Table 10.9 — Insertion of y (huir):**

| Person | Huir 'flee' — Indicative | Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | huyo | huya |
| 2 (tú) | huyes | huyas |
| (vos) [LA] | huís | huyas |
| 3 (él etc.) | huye | huya |
| 1 (nosotros) | huimos | huyamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | huís | huyáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | huyen | huyan |

Other -uir verbs: construir, disminuir, intuir. (*argüir* takes dieresis: *arguyo* but *argüimos*.)

#### 10.1.2.3 Irregular stress (-iar and -uar verbs)

**Rule:** In a large group of **-ar** verbs whose stem ends in **i** or **u** (e.g. *enviar*, *continuar*), the stem vowel is stressed throughout the present indicative and subjunctive **except** in the 1st and 2nd persons plural and the *vos* indicative forms.

**Table 10.10 — -iar and -uar verbs (present indicative):**

| Person | Enviar 'send' | Continuar 'continue' |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | envío | continúo |
| 2 (tú) | envías | continúas |
| (vos) [LA] | enviás | continuás |
| 3 (él etc.) | envía | continúa |
| 1 (nosotros) | enviamos | continuamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | enviáis | continuáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | envían | continúan |

**Table 10.11 — -iar and -uar verbs (present subjunctive):**

| Person | Enviar | Continuar |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | envíe | continúe |
| 2 (tú/vos) | envíes | continúes |
| 3 (él etc.) | envíe | continúe |
| 1 (nosotros) | enviemos | continuemos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | enviéis | continuéis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | envíen | continúen |

Common verbs like **enviar**: confiar, criar, desafiar, desviar, enfriarse, espiar, fiarse, guiar, liar, vaciar, variar.
Common verbs like **continuar**: acentuar, actuar, atenuar, efectuar, evaluar, graduarse, habituar, situar.

*Note:* Most -iar verbs are **regular** (e.g. *cambio*, *cambias*). Verbs in **-cuar** and **-guar** are also regular: *evacuar*, *averiguar*.

**Verbs with a diphthong in the stem** (e.g. *aislar*, *reunir*, *prohibir*): the diphthong splits into two syllables in the singular and 3rd plural of both present indicative and subjunctive.

**Table 10.12/10.13 — Stem diphthong verbs:**

| Person | Aislar 'isolate' — Indic. | Subj. | Reunir 'gather' — Indic. | Subj. | Prohibir 'forbid' — Indic. | Subj. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | aíslo | aísle | reúno | reúna | prohíbo | prohíba |
| 2 (tú) | aíslas | aísles | reúnes | reúnas | prohíbes | prohíbas |
| 3 (él etc.) | aísla | aísle | reúne | reúna | prohíbe | prohíba |
| 1 (nosotros) | aislamos | aislemos | reunimos | reunamos | prohibimos | prohibamos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | aisláis | aisléis | reunís | reunáis | prohibís | prohibáis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | aíslan | aíslen | reúnen | reúnan | prohíben | prohíban |

Verbs that do **not** follow this pattern despite having a diphthong: *arraigarse*, *causar*, *peinar*, *reinar*.

#### 10.1.2.4 Orthography changing verbs

**Rule:** Certain spelling adjustments preserve correct pronunciation when a verb's stem changes the following vowel. These are **not** genuine irregularities but are essential for correct writing.

**-ar verbs** — Changes occur throughout the present subjunctive before **e**:

**Table 10.14 — Spelling changes in -ar verbs:**

| | Llegar 'arrive' | | Cazar 'hunt' | | Sacar 'take out' |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | Indicative | Subjunctive | Indicative | Subjunctive | Indicative | Subjunctive |
| yo | llego | llegue | cazo | cace | saco | saque |
| tú | llegas | llegues | cazas | caces | sacas | saques |
| vos [LA] | llegás | llegues | cazás | caces | sacás | saques |
| él etc. | llega | llegue | caza | cace | saca | saque |
| nosotros | llegamos | lleguemos | cazamos | cacemos | sacamos | saquemos |
| vosotros [SP] | llegáis | lleguéis | cazáis | cacéis | sacáis | saquéis |
| ellos etc. | llegan | lleguen | cazan | cacen | sacan | saquen |

*Note:* **averiguar** changes **gu** > **gü** in the present subjunctive: *averigüe*, *averigües* etc.

**-er and -ir verbs** — Changes occur in 1st person singular present indicative and throughout present subjunctive before **a** or **o**:

**Table 10.15 — Spelling changes in -er/-ir verbs:**

| | Seguir 'follow' — Indic. | Subj. | Vencer 'defeat' — Indic. | Subj. | Recoger 'collect' — Indic. | Subj. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | sigo | siga | venzo | venza | recojo | recoja |
| tú | sigues | sigas | vences | venzas | recoges | recojas |
| él etc. | sigue | siga | vence | venza | recoge | recoja |
| nosotros | seguimos | sigamos | vencemos | venzamos | recogemos | recojamos |
| vosotros [SP] | seguís | sigáis | vencéis | venzáis | recogéis | recojáis |
| ellos etc. | siguen | sigan | vencen | venzan | recogen | recojan |

*Note:* This pattern does not apply to verbs like *aparecer* (see Table 10.8 — those use c > zc).

#### 10.1.2.5 Miscellaneous irregularities

**Rule:** A small group of verbs has idiosyncratic irregularities in the present indicative and/or subjunctive. These must be learned individually.

**Table 10.16 — Idiosyncratic present indicative forms:**

| | Ser 'be' | Estar 'be' | Ir 'go' | Dar 'give' | Ver 'see' | Haber (aux.) | Saber 'know' |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | soy | estoy | voy | doy | veo | he | sé |
| tú | eres | estás | vas | das | ves | has | sabes |
| él etc. | es | está | va | da | ve | ha | sabe |
| nosotros | somos | estamos | vamos | damos | vemos | hemos | sabemos |
| vosotros [SP] | sois | estáis | vais | dais | veis | habéis | sabéis |
| ellos etc. | son | están | van | dan | ven | han | saben |

*Note:* Apart from *ser* (*vos sos*) and *saber* (*vos sabés*), these verbs have no distinct *vos* forms.

**Table 10.17 — Idiosyncratic present subjunctive forms:**

| | Ser | Estar | Ir | Dar | Ver | Haber | Saber |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | sea | esté | vaya | dé | vea | haya | sepa |
| tú | seas | estés | vayas | des | veas | hayas | sepas |
| él etc. | sea | esté | vaya | dé | vea | haya | sepa |
| nosotros | seamos | estemos | vayamos | demos | veamos | hayamos | sepamos |
| vosotros [SP] | seáis | estéis | vayáis | deis | veáis | hayáis | sepáis |
| ellos etc. | sean | estén | vayan | den | vean | hayan | sepan |

**caber** follows the pattern of *saber* but with *quepo* (1st sg. indicative) and *quepa* etc. (subjunctive).

*For the uses of commands, see Chapter 19.*

## **10.4 Preterite tense**

*For uses, see 11.1.2.*

### **10.4.1 Regular preterite forms**

**Formation note:** **-ar**: -é/-aste/-ó/-amos/-asteis/-aron. **-er/-ir**: -í/-iste/-ió/-imos/-isteis/-ieron. *Vos* [LA] usually takes the regular 2nd person singular endings.

### **10.4.2 Irregular preterite forms**

#### 10.4.2.1 Radical changing verbs in the preterite

**Rule:** All **-ir** radical changing verbs show a stem vowel change in the 3rd person singular and plural of the preterite: **e > i** (most verbs) or **o > u** (*morir*, *dormir*).

**Table 10.22 — Radical changes in preterite:**

| Person | Pedir 'ask for' | Dormir 'sleep' |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | pedí | dormí |
| 2 (tú/vos) | pediste | dormiste |
| 3 (él etc.) | pidió | durmió |
| 1 (nosotros) | pedimos | dormimos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | pedisteis | dormisteis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | pidieron | durmieron |

*Note:* In **freír** and **reír**, the **i** from the e > i change absorbs the **i** of the ending: *frió* (not ~~friió~~), *frieron* (not ~~friieron~~).

#### 10.4.2.2 Irregular preterite: stem and stress irregularities

**Rule:** A group of common verbs has stress on the **stem** (not the ending) in 1st and 3rd person singular, plus an unpredictable irregular stem. No forms in this group carry a written accent.

**Table 10.23 — Irregular preterite (stem + stress):**

| Person | Tener 'have' | Venir 'come' | Decir 'say' |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (yo) | tuve | vine | dije |
| 2 (tú/vos) | tuviste | viniste | dijiste |
| 3 (él etc.) | tuvo | vino | dijo |
| 1 (nosotros) | tuvimos | vinimos | dijimos |
| 2 (vosotros) [SP] | tuvisteis | vinisteis | dijisteis |
| 3 (ellos etc.) | tuvieron | vinieron | dijeron |

Verbs like **tener**: andar (*anduve*), caber (*cupe*), estar (*estuve*), haber (*hube*), poder (*pude*), poner (*puse*), saber (*supe*).
Verbs like **venir**: querer (*quise*), hacer (*hice*, but *hizo* 3rd sg.).
Verbs like **decir**: traer (*traje*), producir (*produje*), and all -ducir verbs.

*Notes:*
- The 3rd person plural of this group ends in **-eron** (not -ieron): *tuvieron*, *dijeron*.
- **ir** and **ser** share identical preterite forms: *fui*, *fuiste*, *fue*, *fuimos*, *fuisteis*, *fueron*.
- **dar** and **ver** have a reduced stem (d-, v-) with regular stress: *di*, *dio*; *vi*, *vio*.

## **10.9 Compound tenses**

**Formation note:** Perfect, pluperfect, future perfect and conditional perfect are formed with the appropriate tense of **haber** + past participle.

**Table 10.30 — Compound tenses (hablar):**

| Person | Perfect | Pluperfect | Future perfect | Conditional perfect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 sg. | he hablado | había hablado | habré hablado | habría hablado |
| 2 sg. | has hablado | habías hablado | habrás hablado | habrías hablado |
| 3 sg. | ha hablado | había hablado | habrá hablado | habría hablado |
| 1 pl. | hemos hablado | habíamos hablado | habremos hablado | habríamos hablado |
| 2 pl. [SP] | habéis hablado | habíais hablado | habréis hablado | habríais hablado |
| 3 pl. | han hablado | habían hablado | habrán hablado | habrían hablado |

*The same pattern applies to beber (bebido) and subir (subido), and to all verbs.*

**Subjunctive counterparts:**
- Perfect subjunctive: present subjunctive of haber + participle — *haya hecho*, *hayas hecho* etc.
- Pluperfect subjunctive: imperfect subjunctive of haber + participle — *hubiera/hubiese hecho* etc.

**Perfect infinitive:** *haber* + participle — *después de haber hablado* 'after having spoken'.

**Past anterior** (literary only): preterite of *haber* + participle — *hube hablado*. See 11.2.5.

**Future perfect subjunctive** (virtually extinct): future subjunctive of *haber* + participle — *hubiere hablado*.

**tener** as alternative auxiliary: with transitive verbs, *tener* can replace *haber* to emphasize the result; the participle then agrees with the direct object: *Tengo corregidos los ensayos* 'I've got the essays marked'.
