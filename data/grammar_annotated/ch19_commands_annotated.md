# Chapter 19: Commands

Commands in Spanish can be expressed by **imperative forms** of the verb and by the **present subjunctive**. Users must account for: (i) whether the command is addressed to one or more persons in familiar speech (*tú*, *vosotros/as* [SP], *vos* [LA], *ustedes* [LA]) or (ii) polite speech (*usted*, *ustedes*).

*For the position of object pronouns in commands, see 8.5.1.*

## **19.1 Forms of the imperative**

### **19.1.1 Imperative forms with *tú* and *vosotros* [SP]**

**Rule:** True imperative forms exist only for **2nd person familiar**, and **only for positive commands** (not negative). For negative commands, the present subjunctive is used (see 19.2.2).

- ***Tú* form:** identical to the **3rd person singular of the present indicative** (regular or irregular).
- ***Vosotros* form [SP]:** replace the final **-r** of the infinitive with **-d**. Exception: this **-d is omitted** before the reflexive pronoun **os** (except for *irse → idos*).

**Regular forms:**

| Verb | *Tú* form | *Vosotros* form [SP] | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| avanzar | avanza | avanzad | advance |
| pensar | piensa | pensad | think |
| enviar | envía | enviad | send |
| continuar | continúa | continuad | continue |
| leer | lee | leed | read |
| volver | vuelve | volved | go back |
| subir | sube | subid | go up |
| dormir | duerme | dormid | sleep |
| seguir | sigue | seguid | follow |
| huir | huye | huid | flee |
| acostarse | acuéstate | acostaos | go to bed |
| tenderse | tiéndete | tendeos | stretch out |
| divertirse | diviértete | divertíos | amuse yourself |

**Eight irregular verbs with shortened *tú* form:**

| Verb | *Tú* form | *Vosotros* form [SP] | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| decir | di | decid | tell |
| hacer | haz | haced | do |
| ir | ve | id | go |
| poner | pon | poned | put |
| salir | sal | salid | leave |
| ser | sé | sed | be |
| tener | ten | tened | have |
| venir | ven | venid | come |

*Notes:*
- *Compound verbs adopt the same irregular singular: **detener** → **detén**, **deshacer** → **deshaz**, **proponer** → **propón**. Forms ending in **-n** require a written accent.*
- *The accent on **sé** distinguishes it from the pronoun **se**.*
- ***Estar** singular is often rendered as reflexive **estate**: **¡estate tranquilo!** 'Be quiet'.*
- *Occasionally **valer** is shortened to **val**.*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Juan, cierra la puerta. | Juan, close the door. |
| Tenme preparada la cena. | Have dinner ready for me. |
| Colecciónalos. | Collect them. |
| Tomad lo que queráis. [SP] | Take whatever you want. |
| Limpiaos los zapatos. [SP] | Wipe your shoes. |

### **19.1.2 Imperative forms with *vos* [LA]**

**Rule:** In regions where **vos** is used (especially Argentina), the familiar singular imperative is formed by **dropping the final -d from the *vosotros* form**. A **written accent** is placed over the final vowel — except for monosyllables *¡da!* and *¡ve!*. The accent is generally retained when a pronoun is added.

**Forms (compared to *tú*):**

| Verb | *Vos* form [LA] | Corresponding *tú* form |
|---|---|---|
| buscar | buscá | busca |
| probar | probá | prueba |
| vender | vendé | vende |
| volver | volvé | vuelve |
| vivir | viví | vive |
| dormir | dormí | duerme |
| seguir | seguí | sigue |
| tener | tené | ten |
| decir | decí | di |
| acostarse | acostáte | acuéstate |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| María, vení lo más pronto posible. | María, come as soon as you can. |
| Convertíte en estrella. | Turn yourself into a star. |
| Casáte con él. | Marry him. |
| Hacé click sobre el ícono. | Click on the icon. |
| Ingresá tu usuario y clave. | Enter your username and password. |
| Sentáte y calláte. | Sit down and be quiet. |

## **19.2 Commands which use the present subjunctive**

**Rule:** The present subjunctive is required for:
- **All commands** addressed to *usted* and *ustedes* (2nd/3rd person subjunctive)
- **All negative commands** (familiar or polite)
- **3rd person commands** after *que*
- **1st person plural commands** ('let's')

### **19.2.1 Commands addressed to *usted* and *ustedes***

**Rule:** The **3rd person of the present subjunctive** is required for polite commands (*usted/ustedes*), and in Latin America where *ustedes* also replaces *vosotros* in familiar speech. The subject pronoun may be added after the verb for emphasis.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dígame lo que quiere hacer. | Tell me what you want to do. |
| Suban ustedes al próximo autobús. | Get on the next bus. |
| Mis amigos, déjenlo aquí. [LA] | My friends, leave it here. |

### **19.2.2 Negative commands**

**Rule:** The **2nd or 3rd person of the present subjunctive** (as appropriate) is used for **all** negative commands — familiar or polite. Object pronouns **precede** the verb in negative commands.

**Contrast with positive commands:**

| Positive | Negative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Hazlo | No lo hagas | Do it / Don't do it (*tú*) |
| Hágalo | No lo haga | Do it / Don't do it (*usted*) |
| Hacedlo [SP] | No lo hagáis [SP] | Do it / Don't do it (*vosotros*) |
| Háganlo | No lo hagan | Do it / Don't do it (*ustedes*) |

*Note: In regions using **vos**, use the standard 2nd person singular present subjunctive for negative commands: **No me toques (vos)** 'Don't touch me'.*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No critiques a los que no están. | Don't criticize those who are not here. |
| No penséis jamás que fue así. [SP] | Never think it was like that. |
| No lo confiese ahora. | Don't confess it now. |
| No salgan antes de que llegue. | Do not leave before I arrive. |

### **19.2.3 1st person plural commands — 'let's'**

**Rule:** The **1st person plural of the present subjunctive** expresses 'let's' / 'let us'. With reflexive verbs, the final **-s** is dropped before **nos**: *sentemos + nos → sentémonos*. In informal speech, replaced by **ir a** + infinitive. Exception: *ir* and *irse* use present tense forms *vamos* and *vámonos* (not *vayamos/vayámonos*).

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Bailemos. | Let's dance. |
| Corramos. | Let's run. |
| Despidámonos ahora. | Let's take our leave now. |
| Vamos a cenar en otro sitio. | Let's have dinner somewhere else. (informal) |
| Vamos. | Let's go. |
| Vámonos. | Let's go away / Let's get out of here. |

### **19.2.4 Commands introduced by *que***

**Rule:** **3rd person commands** are expressed with **que** + singular or plural of the present subjunctive. The English equivalent often begins with 'let'. 2nd person commands may also be conveyed in this way (more indirect/informal).

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Que nadie hable durante el examen. | There should be no talking during the examination. |
| Que discutan el proyecto mañana. | Let them discuss the plan tomorrow. |
| Que tengáis una feliz Navidad. [SP] | Have a happy Christmas. |
| Que te diviertas. | Have a good time. |

## **19.3 Alternative ways of expressing commands**

### **19.3.1 Softened commands**

**Rule:** While native speakers use direct commands more readily than English speakers, softening formulas exist. Commands can also be expressed as questions.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¡Háganme el favor de sentarse! | Please sit down. |
| ¡Firme aquí, por favor! | Sign here, please! |
| ¿Podría apagar la tele? | Would you switch off the TV? |
| ¿Me da un poquito de pan? | Would you give me some bread? |

### **19.3.2 Infinitive, present and future tenses as commands**

**Rule:** Three non-imperative verb forms can express commands in specific contexts:

**(a) Infinitive** — increasingly common for notices and written instructions (especially negative). In Spain also replaces the *vosotros* **-d** form in speech. Preceded by **a**, functions as a 1st person plural command.

**(b) Present tense** — expresses curt, sometimes blunt commands. Common among friends.

**(c) Future tense** — formulates authoritative, dogmatic commands.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Form |
|---|---|---|
| No escupir. | No spitting. | infinitive — notice |
| Tachar la respuesta que no corresponda. | Cross out the incorrect answer. | infinitive — instruction |
| Limpiaros los dientes antes de acostaros. [SP] | Clean your teeth before going to bed. | infinitive replacing *-d* form [SP] |
| Y ahora muchachos, a trabajar. | Now lads, let's get to work. | a + infinitive — 1st person pl. |
| Vas allá y les dices que no. [LA] | You go there and tell them no. | present tense — curt |
| Lo comes ahora, o te lo quito. | Eat it now or I'll take it away. | present tense — curt |
| ¡No lo abrirás hasta que no te lo diga! | You won't open it until I say so! | future — dogmatic |

### **19.3.3 Impersonal commands**

**Rule:** The **3rd person singular or plural of the subjunctive** with the reflexive pronoun **se** is a common way of writing formal instructions or directions.

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Después de abrir la lata, guárdese en la nevera. | After opening the tin, keep in the fridge. |
| Pónganse las almendras en la sartén y déjense dorar. | Place the almonds in the frying pan and let them brown. |
