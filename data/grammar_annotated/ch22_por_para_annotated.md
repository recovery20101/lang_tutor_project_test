# Chapter 22: *Por* and *para*

Both *por* and *para* can translate English 'for', but they are rarely interchangeable without changing meaning.

**Core distinction:**
- **Por** → looks *back* towards the **source** of things (where things come from, cause, reason, agent, time/place *through which*)
- **Para** → refers to the **destination** of things (where things are going, purpose, deadline, recipient)

**Illustrative contrast:**

| Spanish | English | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| El documento fue preparado **por** el consejo **para** el rey. | The document was prepared **by** the council **for** the king. | *por* = source/agent; *para* = destination/recipient |
| Vamos a París **por** un mes. | We are going to Paris for a month. | *por* = time *during which* |
| Mañana salgo **para** Sevilla. | Tomorrow I leave **for** Seville. | *para* = destination |
| Va a Sevilla **por** Madrid. | He is going to Seville **via** Madrid. | *por* = place *through which* |
| Debemos terminarlo **para** mañana. | We must finish it **by** tomorrow. | *para* = deadline/destination in time |

## **22.1 Uses of *por***

**Rule:** *Por* has thirteen main uses, summarised below.

**(a) Agent in passive constructions** — 'by':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Fue invadido por los romanos. | It was invaded by the Romans. |

**(b) Reason or cause** — 'because of', 'on account of', 'through', 'for'. Note: *Lo hice por ti* = 'I did it because of/on account of you' (reason), NOT 'I did it for you' (purpose → use *para ti*).*

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gusta por lo fácil que es. | I like it because of how easy it is. |
| Lo admiro por su paciencia. | I admire him for his patience. |
| Por estar enferma no pudo venir. | Through being ill she could not come. |

**(c) Time during/throughout which** — often 'for'. When the verb's action takes place for the *entire* period, use **durante** (or no preposition) instead: *Voy a hablar durante una hora* 'I will speak for one hour'; *Estuve en Londres dos días* 'I was in London for two days'.*

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Piensa venir por un mes. | He intends coming for a month. |

**(d) Movement through a place** — 'through', 'via', 'along':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tuvimos que salir por la ventana. | We had to go out via the window. |
| Caminaba por la selva. | I was walking through the jungle. |

**(e) Imprecise location or time** — 'around', 'in that area':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Está por ahí. | It's around somewhere. |
| Nunca se le ve por la universidad. | You never see him around the university. |
| No se hacía por entonces. | People did not do it at that time. |

*For **por la tarde** 'in the afternoon', see 23.1.5.1.*

**(f) Manner or means** — 'by', 'by means of':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| por teléfono / carretera / correo electrónico | by phone / road / e-mail |

**(g) Exchange** — 'for':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo compré por 500 euros. | I bought it for 500 euros. |
| Cambió su regalo por otro. | She changed her present for another. |

**(h) On behalf of / in favour of / in support of** — 'for', 'on behalf of':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No lo hagas por mí. | Don't do it just for me. |
| El está aquí por Andrea. | He's here on behalf of Andrea. |
| Estoy por los derechos indígenas. | I am in favour of indigenous rights. |

**(i) Personal opinion or evaluation** — 'for', 'as':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo tomé por otro. | I took him for someone else. |
| Podrían pasar por hermanas. | They could pass for sisters. |

**(j) Rate and quantity** — 'a', 'per', 'by the':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Viene siete veces por semana. | He comes seven times a week. |
| Es más económico comprarlos por kilo. | It is more economical to buy them by the kilo. |

**(k) What remains to be done** — *por* + infinitive:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me quedan tres páginas por fotocopiar. | I have three pages left to photocopy. |
| Está todo por discutir. | Everything is still open for discussion. |

**(l) Objective of a movement verb** — 'for' (to go and get). *In the Iberian Peninsula, **a** is usually inserted before **por**: **Ha ido a por pan**.*

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ha ido por pan. | He has gone for some bread. |
| Vinieron por la televisión. | They came for the television. |

**(m) Multiplication:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Cinco por dos son diez. | Five times two is ten. |

## **22.2 Uses of *para***

**Rule:** *Para* has four main uses.

**(a) Destination** — place, person as intended receiver/beneficiary, function:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mañana salgo para Guayaquil. | Tomorrow I am leaving for Guayaquil. |
| unos libros para ti | some books for you |
| Están estudiando para el examen. | They are studying for the examination. |
| ¿Para qué sirve este instrumento? | What is this instrument for? |

**(b) Deadline or target in time** — 'for', 'by', 'until':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Para el domingo estará completo. | By Sunday it will be complete. |
| Tengo una cita para el sábado. | I have an appointment for Saturday. |
| Aplazó la charla para la semana que viene. | He put off the talk until next week. |

**(c) Purpose** — *para* + infinitive = '(in order) to':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Se necesita mucho dinero para hacerlo. | You need a lot of money to do it. |
| Lo dijo para impresionar a la gente. | He said it in order to impress people. |
| un trapo para limpiar cristales | a cloth for cleaning windows |

**(d) Unexpected inequality / comparison** — 'for', 'although', 'considering', 'despite':

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Para un francés habla bien portugués. | For a Frenchman he speaks Portuguese well. |
| Es bastante maduro para lo joven que es. | He's quite mature considering how young he is. |
| Es mucho palacio para poca justicia. | It is a large palace despite so little justice. |

### CMP: *Por* vs *para* — full contrast

**Rule:** The clearest way to choose between *por* and *para* is to identify whether the sentence focuses on the **source/reason/means** (*por*) or the **destination/purpose/deadline** (*para*).

| Meaning | *Por* | *Para* |
|---|---|---|
| Agent (passive) | ✓ *por los romanos* | ✗ |
| Cause / reason | ✓ *por su paciencia* | ✗ |
| Time *during which* | ✓ *por un mes* | ✗ |
| Deadline / target time | ✗ | ✓ *para el domingo* |
| Movement *through* | ✓ *por Madrid* | ✗ |
| Movement *towards* (destination) | ✗ | ✓ *para Sevilla* |
| Exchange | ✓ *por 500 euros* | ✗ |
| On behalf of | ✓ *por mí* | ✗ |
| Purpose ('in order to') | ✗ | ✓ *para hacerlo* |
| Recipient / beneficiary | ✗ | ✓ *para ti* |
| Imprecise location | ✓ *por ahí* | ✗ |
| Means ('by') | ✓ *por teléfono* | ✗ |
| What remains to be done | ✓ *por fotocopiar* | ✗ |
| Rate / quantity | ✓ *por semana* | ✗ |
| Comparison / considering | ✗ | ✓ *para un francés* |

**Common confusion — 'I did it for you':**
- *Lo hice **por** ti* → 'I did it because of you / on your account' (cause/reason)
- *Lo hice **para** ti* → 'I did it for you' (as a beneficiary / destination)

**Purpose: *para* vs *por* + infinitive:** *Para* + infinitive is the standard for purpose. *Por* + infinitive also occurs, but implies **cause or motive** rather than pure purpose, often meaning 'because of wanting to': *Decidieron no salir por (querer) estar conmigo* 'They decided not to go out, to be with me'.

## **22.3 *Por* and *para* with *estar***

**Rule:** Both combine with *estar* but with distinct meanings — and regional variation.

| Form | Meaning | Region | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **estar para** + infinitive | about to do, on the point of | Spain | *Estoy para salir.* — I am about to leave. |
| **estar por** + infinitive | inclined to do | both | *Estaba por decir que no.* — I was inclined to say no. |
| **estar por** + infinitive | about to do (= *estar para* [SP]) | Latin America | *Está por llegar.* [LA] — He is about to arrive. |

*Note: In Latin America, **estar por** frequently conveys the 'about to' meaning that **estar para** has in Spain.*

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Estoy para salir. [SP] | I am about to leave. | *estar para* — Spain |
| El tren está para partir. [SP] | The train is on the point of departing. | *estar para* — Spain |
| Estaba por decir que no se podía hacer. | I was inclined to say that it could not be done. | *estar por* — inclination |
